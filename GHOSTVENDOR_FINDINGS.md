## Attempted Patches
- **Stripe**: fix(stripe): add timeout and retry to create_payment_intent

## What Failed
Stripe HTTP calls had no timeout, causing indefinite hangs when the vendor was slow or unresponsive. Transient errors like 429 and 502 were not retried, so a single failure broke the entire request.

## Fix Applied
Added a 10-second timeout to the requests.post call and wrapped it in a retry loop. On timeout or transient status codes (429, 502, 503), the call is retried once after a 1-second delay. On final failure, a structured error dict is returned instead of raising.

## Why This Strategy
Timeout with retry prevents hanging requests and handles transient failures gracefully. The caller was patched to check for error dicts, avoiding KeyError crashes and returning a proper 503 response.
